Self-aligned intelligence: Clerro

People don't need more AI tools — they need systems that know who they are and how they think. As the sole designer on a 0-to-1 AI decision-intelligence product, I turned an abstract idea into an investor-ready prototype: screenshot a product, get a clear, personal verdict you can trust.

01 — Context and opportunity

Decision fatigue and an AI trust gap

  • Problem: decision fatigue as a cognitive burden, and an AI trust gap — 65% of organizations adopt AI, but personalization and trust stay low.
  • Behavioral signal: half of Gen Z save product screenshots — an unmet need hiding in plain sight.
  • Users: consumers at moments of decision friction first; enterprises later, through a decision-intelligence API.
02 — AI-native workflow

Designing with AI, end to end

  1. 01Problem framingAI-assisted synthesis of decision fatigue, the trust gap and screenshot behavior.
  2. 02Insight extractionUser journeys and behavioral patterns: zero-friction entry, progressive disclosure.
  3. 03Rapid prototypingAI-generated variations and testing, leading to an interactive Clarity Card prototype.
  4. 04System designA three-layer system, design specs and a mobile-first design system.
03 — Solution architecture

Three layers of trust

  1. Insight — the default viewBuilds confidence and context: the CQ score and confidence level, why Clerro recommends it, and how it fits you.
  2. Proof — evidence on demandEnables transparency and auditability: verification sources, a data-consistency check, and the insight model used.
  3. Personal CQ — an evolving profileBuilds learning and trust over time: how your clarity evolves, decision patterns and suggestions to improve.
04 — Interaction flow

From screenshot to clarity

Step 1 — Zero-friction entry

On Amazon, TikTok or any store, the user takes a screenshot or shares a link to Clerro. Analysis starts immediately — no login, no onboarding.

Step 2 — The Clarity Card

A moment of friction becomes a moment of confidence: the verdict and CQ score appear at once, with the reasons one tap away.

Step 3 — Transparency on demand

Users can see exactly how a verdict was verified, then explore how their own clarity profile is evolving.

Proof details: data-consistency check and the insight model used.

05 — Key design decisions

Built around how people already behave

  1. Zero-friction entryUsers see value before any commitment; Personal CQ learns quietly through use.
  2. Screenshot as primary inputHalf of Gen Z already screenshot products — no app-switching, and it works across any platform, online or offline.
  3. Mobile-first architecture“People already use screenshots to share things they are looking at on their phones.”
Future expansion

From consumer to enterprise

The same behavioral learning engine can power a different application layer: a ClerroLayer API offering decision logic as a service, and Team CQ for organizational decision patterns — for procurement, vendor selection and compliance decisions.

06 — Key contributions

What I brought

  • Abstract to tangible: translated ambiguous business ideas into concrete screen flows and logical structures.
  • AI-driven UX: established interaction principles for AI uncertainty that build user confidence.
  • Rapid prototyping: high-fidelity prototypes to align engineering and validate concepts.
  • Scalable design system: tokens, components and patterns documented for handoff and future growth.

02 — Approach

How I work on hard B2B problems

Systems thinking

Mapping business goals, personas and data flows across systems before pixels — so the product vision holds up on the roadmap, not just in the demo.

Trustworthy AI

Reusable human–AI patterns — confidence signaling, explainability, evidence transparency and human oversight — so people know when to rely on the model.

Systems that scale

Governing SAP Fiori adoption and mentoring junior and senior designers, keeping complex workflows consistent across teams and releases.

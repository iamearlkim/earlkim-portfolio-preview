01 — The problem

A 2025 deadline and a footprint living in spreadsheets

Samsung Electronics had committed to carbon neutrality by 2050, but the nearer pressure was regulatory: mandatory ESG carbon disclosure (EU CSRD) starting in 2025.

Scope 3 — 12 categories and more than 105M tCO₂e — was tracked entirely by hand. Product life-cycle (LCA) data existed only for representative models. The inputs sat in six disconnected systems: N-ERP, G-EHS, logistics, HR, travel and the supplier portal.

No single interface served the four groups who needed it, each with very different data literacy: executives, ESG managers, plant operators and supply-chain teams.

05 — Outcome

A PoC that turned into two contracts

The proof of concept was completed and converted into two follow-on engagements: enterprise carbon-emission management and product carbon-footprint (LCA) management.

It gave Samsung Electronics its first platform-based, systematic approach to carbon management at global enterprise scale — replacing manual tracking of a 105M+ tCO₂e Scope 3 footprint.

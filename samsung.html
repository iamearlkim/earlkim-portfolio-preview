The challenge

One company, many systems, many personas

Samsung's operations run on many SAP and legacy systems, each serving different teams — executives, ESG managers, plant operators, supply-chain planners and e-commerce operators.

Across engagements, the recurring problem was the same: data lived in disconnected systems and spreadsheets, and the people who needed it worked in very different ways.

My approach

What connected the work

  • Partner with domain experts — learn legacy workflows from EHS, supply-chain and commerce specialists before designing.
  • Design the data flow, not just the screens — define how information should move across systems and roles.
  • Keep it consistent — build on SAP Fiori so each new module feels like part of one platform.

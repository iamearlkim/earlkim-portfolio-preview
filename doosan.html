AI recruitment management for Doosan

Doosan Group was losing employees who wanted to change careers internally. I led the design of an internal hiring system that cuts recruiters' screening time with machine learning and makes internal opportunities visible to employees.

01 — The challenge

High turnover, and no internal path

92% of employees who wanted a career change left the company, and 37% of employees left within three years of an internal transfer. The turnover drove up recruitment costs and dissatisfaction among transferred employees.

02 — Research

What were the right problems?

  1. Lack of systemApplicants had no way to discover and apply for internal positions or see job details.
  2. Manual screeningRecruiters screened candidates in spreadsheets and built reports by hand, stretching processing time.
  3. Lack of privacyRecruiters needed a referral from the candidate's manager, causing tension in the workplace.
How I got there

Workshops, interviews and synthesis

I ran an exploration workshop to scope the project, user interviews with employees and recruiters, and a discover workshop to synthesize the data and define the right problems.

How might we build a healthy, sustainable internal hiring culture through technology?

  • Research goals: understand the unofficial transfer process, and pain points for employees and recruiters.
  • Limitations: all sessions were remote; hiring managers and employees whose transfer failed were not included.
03 — Design goals

Make internal jobs visible, reduce recruiters' paperwork and calls, and protect application data from exposure.

04 — Prototyping and iterations

We used machine learning to give recruiters insightful first-screening results — making the tedious first screen three times shorter. Co-creation sessions, rapid prototypes and user validation shaped each iteration.

AI screening: insight into potential

Recruiters see a machine-learning ranking score for each candidate, a score for each evaluation criterion, and can shortlist, reject or export to Excel from one list.

05 — Outcome

The results after deployment

  • Employee retention increased by 32%.
  • Recruiter productivity increased 2.8×.
  • The hiring process sped up 1.4×.
Next

Improvements for the next phase

  • Design a third, visual level of explanation — from what (score) to why (factors) to how (expert detail).
  • Auto-generate job postings from business demand, task volume and budget, connected to LinkedIn and the talent pool.
06 — What I learned

Reflections

Designing an AI system means weighing data volume, integration and AI ethics. Used well, AI frees people from repetitive work for more creative tasks — and it creates new interactions that make users feel differently about their work.
